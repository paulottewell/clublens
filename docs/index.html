How it works

Three steps to a full picture

No setup, no configuration, no accounts. ClubLens is a single HTML file that runs entirely inside your web browser.

01

Export from Bromcom

Run your standard clubs data export from your school's MIS. ClubLens reads the file exactly as Bromcom produces it — no reformatting needed.

02

Drag and drop

Open ClubLens in any browser and drag your export file onto the page. Processing happens instantly, entirely within your browser.

03

See your picture

Every tab, chart, and figure generates immediately. Participation broken down by the groups that matter — ready for your next governors' report or pupil premium strategy.
